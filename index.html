Privacy

Your files are processed directly in your browser whenever possible. Files used by the image tools are not uploaded to our server. ToolBox Pro does not collect personal data and does not include analytics.

Only your theme, favorite tool IDs and recently used tool IDs are stored in your browser's local storage. Nothing else is saved.

Privacy Policy

ToolBox Pro runs in your browser. Text, files and images you enter into a tool are processed on your device and are not sent to us. We store no accounts and no personal data. Your browser keeps a theme choice, favorite tool IDs and recent tool IDs in local storage; you can clear them at any time in your browser settings. If advertising is enabled, ad providers such as Google may use cookies under their own policies.
